# Content guide

Voice: calm, senior, specific and business-first. Describe the workflow, controls, users and measures before technology. Use “Solution Blueprint” for illustrative capability. Never invent proof or present hypothetical outcomes as completed client results.

Every publishable page needs a unique title, description, one H1, a natural Spanish counterpart and a useful call to action. Add content through the typed modules rather than duplicating route components. Keep public prices non-specific unless approved. Legal content must not guess the contracting entity, registered office or jurisdiction.
