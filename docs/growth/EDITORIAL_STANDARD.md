# Growth editorial standard

## Publication gate

Every new acquisition asset starts with `publishApproved: false`. It cannot enter the sitemap or become indexable until a human reviewer confirms all of the following:

- the page answers a real decision better than a generic summary;
- English and Spanish meaning, limitations and routes remain equivalent;
- no client, partner, result, certification, office, price or legal fact has been invented;
- dependencies and cases where AI is the wrong answer are visible;
- any illustration is labelled as illustrative;
- sources are primary or authoritative where a factual external claim needs support;
- internal links and the next step match the visitor’s stage;
- the page has been checked on mobile, desktop, keyboard and reduced motion;
- metadata, canonical and reciprocal hreflang are correct;
- the reviewer records the approval and review date in the content definition.

## Writing standard

Lead with the immediate answer. Explain what changes that answer, what commonly fails and what evidence a decision requires. Prefer concrete workflow language—owners, systems, sources, handoffs, exceptions and measures—over AI category jargon.

Do not use arbitrary word counts, generated FAQ farms, hidden keyword variants or schema markup as ranking tactics. Visible questions exist to help people. `FAQPage` and `QAPage` schema are not added to these commercial pages.

## Proof standard

When approved proof does not exist, describe a capability or clearly labelled illustrative workflow. Never imply a customer result. Record future proof using: context, baseline, implementation boundary, measurement method, period, outcome, limitations and written permission.

## Tool standard

Calculators must expose their formula and assumptions, avoid sensational defaults and separate released capacity from cash savings. Diagnostics must explain their rules, show useful output before lead capture and avoid scientific, predictive or guaranteed language.

## Review cadence

Review priority assets after material product/process changes and at least every six months. Consolidate overlapping content rather than creating a second page for the same intent.
